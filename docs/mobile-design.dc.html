Empty and error states centre in the column and their action becomes full width — the only place a secondary button stretches. Skeletons keep the same paper tones as 10 · 01. The fade sits at the right edge of every scrolling chip row.

→ Breakpoint at 720px. Below it: one column, 20pt gutters, controls at 48pt and tap targets never under 44pt. Above it the desktop layouts in 01–10 apply unchanged.

→ Sticky footers use env(safe-area-inset-bottom) and sit on translucent paper with a hairline top border, so content scrolls visibly beneath them.

→ Sorting is never demoted on mobile: the Newest / Top pair stays a visible segmented control on both the public board and the dashboard — not folded into a menu.

→ The state sheet in 10 is layout-independent and applies at both widths, so it has no separate mobile treatment here.

→ Implementation: real button, a, label, input, select and fieldset elements throughout — no div acting as a control. Focus is visible on every interactive element (2px brass ring, offset 2px) and never removed on mouse users. Validation messages are tied to their field with aria-describedby; a single polite aria-live region per screen announces vote, status, save and create results. Status is carried by its text label and its distinct symbol, never by colour alone. Functional text stays at 12px or above, and mobile targets at 44pt or above.
